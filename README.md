(a) Problem statement

Online stores get many visits but most end without many purchases. That means huge cost to retailer in terms of lost reveue and wasted marketing spend. Given what we can observe about a visit (pages viewed, time spent, bounce and exit behaviour, traffic source, month), can we predict whether it will end in a purchase so that retailer can maximise the revenue from online stores. With a reliable prediction of real buyer, retailer can offer discount prompt for a visit that looks likely to buy and not waste an offer on one that won’t. Only about 15.5% of visits convert, so the buyers are rare and easy to overlook. A prediction that ignores them would look accurate and be useless.
